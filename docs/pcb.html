HDDSynthPro PCB

Every connection on the board: an RP2350B, with Raspberry Pi's minimal-design support circuit, acting as an IDE drive behind four 5 V-tolerant bus switches, with USB-C and a 5 V header for power, the activity link to the HDD Synth, a microSD socket and the activity LED. The GPIO numbers are the ones in src/config.h, so the drive firmware runs unchanged, apart from the new pins marked NEW.

Never join the IDE side's 5 V to this board

The adapter's Molex power stays off the PCB. The two boards share ground through the IDE connector only.

The bus switches start open

BUS_EN# is pulled high, so the IDE bus is disconnected until firmware drives GP41 low. Close JP1 to run today's firmware, which doesn't know about GP41.

IORDY is still open drain

It passes through the switch like any other line. Firmware only pulls it low or releases it; the host pulls it up to 5 V and the switch caps what the RP2350 sees at about 3.3 V.

Check before ordering

The regulator inductor (L1, Abracon AOTA-B201610S3R3-101-T) was out of stock at JLCPCB on 27 Oct 2026: source it before ordering. In JLCPCB's placement preview, L1's polarity dot must sit at its 1.1 V end, away from the LX pin.

Notes for layout

IDE bus

  • Keep U1 → U2, U3, U5, U6 → J5 short, and the bus on one layer over a solid ground plane.
  • Fit R14 and R15 (33 Ω, DIOR#/DIOW#) between U1 and U3, close to U1.
  • Join every IDE ground pin to the plane. Leave the key (pin 20) out of the header.
  • No pull-ups or pull-downs on the bus: the host provides them, and the RP2350's internal pull-downs stay off (erratum E9).
  • The channels follow the header's pin order, so the IDE side runs straight. Spare channels (U6 A1–A4/B1–B4) stay unconnected.

Power

  • D1, D2 and D5 diode-OR USB, the 5 V header and the link socket's pin 1 into VB, and block reverse polarity. Pin 1 is for a future HDD Synth that powers both boards from one supply. VB sits about 0.35 V below the input; the AMS1117 holds 3.3 V down to about 4.4 V on VB.
  • The RP2350B's core regulator, crystal and flash copy Raspberry Pi's layout exactly, including its 0.125 mm spacing inside that area (a design rule allows it there only).
  • Put a 100 nF next to each switch's VCC pin (C4, C5, C7, C8), and C2/C3 at the SD socket.

Firmware changes this board brings

  • GP41 BUS_EN#: drive low once the bus program is set up (or close JP1).
  • GP40: SD card detect, for re-mounting a card put back while running.
  • GP31 and GP3: DMARQ and DMACK#, routed now so DMA needs no new board.
  • The pin rules in CLAUDE.md, config.h and the wiring docs would gain a line for each.
