Adafruit microSD breakout to the BB48

Six signal wires, 3.3 V and ground, plus five 10 kΩ pull-ups. CLK, CMD and DAT0 land on the RP2350's SPI0 pins, DAT3 is the SPI chip select, and DAT0–DAT3 sit on consecutive pins, so the same wiring later runs in 4-bit SDIO mode. Pick any pin or row to see its wire.

Everything unplugged while soldering

Adapter USB out first, then the BB48. The SD card has nothing to do with the IDE bus, so the power-order rule is unchanged.

The breakout has no pull-ups

Fit 10 kΩ from CMD, DAT0, DAT1, DAT2 and DAT3 to 3.3 V, close to the breakout. CLK needs none: the RP2350 always drives it.

3 V only

Power it from the BB48's +3.3V (EXT1-1). Never from VBUS or VSYS: those are 5 V and would damage the card.

Before power

  • Continuity: each breakout pin beeps to its BB48 pin in the table.
  • No beep between neighbouring pins EXT2-14 to EXT2-19, or from any of them to GND or 3.3 V.
  • Resistance (Ω, 20 kΩ range): each of CMD and DAT0–DAT3 reads about 10 kΩ to the breakout's 3V pin.
  • Keep the signal wires short, ideally under 10 cm: SPI runs at 12–25 MHz, SDIO faster.
  • Leave DET (card detect) unconnected for now.

First check

With the BB48 on USB and a FAT32 microSD (up to 32 GB) in the slot, type S in the serial console. It only reads the card.

SD ok type=SDHC/SDXC size_mb=30436 fs=FAT32
  • SD error: no card, or it doesn't answer: check the card is in, then the CLK, CMD, DAT0 and DAT3 wires.
  • fs=none: the card answers but isn't FAT; format it FAT32 on the Mac.
